# Mechanics of Power

`"Power tends to corrupt and absolute power corrupts absolutely. Great men are almost always bad men, even when they exercise influence and not authority; still more when you superadd the tendency of the certainty of corruption by authority.” -Lord Acton`

The more power someone has, the greater that their influence can be. Good or evil. Unfortunately mankind tends toward selfishness and evil, so the longer that someone has power, the more likely it is that they will use it for an evil purpose
